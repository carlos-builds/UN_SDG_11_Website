Local spotlight: Trinidad & Tobago

Port of Spain is the country's largest urban centre, with an estimated population of roughly 478,000 in its metro area in 2025, followed by Chaguanas and San Fernando. Urban resilience challenges here mirror the global pattern.

A recurring pattern: flooding and gridlock downtown

Local news coverage over the past two years has repeatedly documented the same scene: heavy rainfall causing street flooding on routes like Wrightson Road, South Quay, and Independence Square, followed by hours of standstill traffic as commuters try to leave the capital. The Ministry of Works and Infrastructure has linked the worsening pattern to ageing drainage systems, clogged waterways, and tidal action — exactly the kind of infrastructure gap SDG 11 targets.
